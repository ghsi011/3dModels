# Bench test protocol — water, kitchen, BEFORE any hive install

Use plain water (behaves like syrup for sealing; syrup only changes viscosity/density
slightly). Assemble body + gasket + nut on a scrap board with a Ø44 hole, or hold the
body upright in a pot.

## 1. Regulation cycle (10 min)

1. Fill a 1 L bottle full, screw it in over a dry tray.
2. **Expect:** a burst of glugs while the pool rises, then silence with the pool at the
   outlet lip (~4.5 mm). Bottle flanks should visibly panel inward.
3. Spoon water out of the tray → the feeder must answer with a glug or two and re-level,
   then go quiet again. Repeat 3–4 times.
4. FAIL if it trickles continuously or the pool passes the boss tops while standing.

## 2. 24 h leak watch (overnight)

1. Refill, mark the bottle level with tape, note the pool level.
2. After 24 h undisturbed: bottle level unchanged (evaporation-scale only), pool at the
   lip, floor around the rig dry.
3. Any measurable bottle drop that reappeared in the tray = an air leak: gasket seat or
   barrel wall. Retighten and repeat; if it persists, reprint body at flow ×1.04 or brush
   food-safe epoxy inside the bore.

## 3. Thermal test (the §3.7 killer, 30 min)

1. Run the bottle down to ~1/4 full (worst case: max headspace), let it stabilize.
2. Hair-dryer the bottle body on low from ~30 cm, cycling 5 min on / 5 min off, 3 cycles
   (or stand it in full sun for an afternoon).
3. **Expect:** bottle un-panels/bulges, pool rises some mm, possibly one burp — but the
   level must stay below the rim with clear freeboard, and it must recede as it cools.
4. FAIL only if water tops the rim → re-check flood reserve assumptions before hive use.

## 4. Bottle-swap rehearsal (5 min)

Practice the swap from `assembly_field_instructions.md` with water until the invert-and-
screw motion is one smooth 2-turn commit. Confirm: spill lands in the tray (not past the
rim), plug goes in/out with gloves on.

## 5. Clamp extremes (once, with the printed parts)

Clamp the feeder on 8 mm plywood and on a 30 mm stack: nut reaches firm hand-tight on
both, tray stays rigid under a sideways shove of the bottle (simulated wind), nothing
creaks. Leave the 30 mm setup loaded overnight in the sun with a full bottle — re-check
snugness (expected: slight relaxation, one re-snug).
