# Assembly & field instructions — Nuc bottle feeder

## Parts

body (tray+barrel) · clamp nut · TPU gasket washer · transport plug · 1 L PCO-1881 still-
water bottle (the feeder fits any bottle with this neck — nearly every Israeli 0.5/1/1.5 L
still bottle).

## Hive prep (bench, roof panel off the hive)

1. Pick the spot: the tray is Ø150 — keep its center ≥ 90 mm from hive walls so the rim
   gap stays open, roughly over the frame seams you want to feed.
2. **Drill Ø44 mm** (hole saw) straight through roof + ceiling as one hole. Range Ø42–46
   works; the clamp faces (Ø60 below, Ø80 above) cover any of these. Deburr.
3. Drop the **gasket** into the socket (flat washer onto the internal shoulder — poke it
   flat with a finger).

## Install (no tools)

4. From below, push the barrel up through the hole (tray in hand).
5. From above, spin the **nut** down the barrel (2-start thread ≈ 3 turns) and hand-
   tighten firmly while holding the tray. The skirt should end up ~3 mm above the roof —
   it must NOT touch (if it does, your roof is thicker than 30 mm).
6. Screw in the **plug** until the hive is populated with a bottle. The hive is now
   bee-tight, rain-shedding, and structurally done.
7. **Anti-ant:** smear a finger of Vaseline/barrier grease around the barrel *under the
   skirt*. Renew monthly.
8. Works clamped anywhere in **8–30 mm** roof thickness; re-snug the nut at your first
   inspection (plastic seats into wood) and at routine visits.

## Filling / bottle swap (gloved, one hand per step)

1. Fill the bottle with 1:1 syrup **to the brim** (less headspace = less thermal
   push-out), cap it with its own cap, carry to the hive.
2. Unscrew the plug (or spent bottle) — slowly; the barrel column (~15 ml) drains into
   the tray. Set it down on the roof.
3. Uncap the fresh bottle, invert it over the socket, and screw it in — **commit: one
   smooth motion, ~2 turns.** It will glug ~30 ml while the threads engage; the tray is
   sized to swallow that. Tighten to a firm hand stop (that's the gasket compressing).
4. Watch 30 s: the bottle should glug a few times, panel inward slightly, then go quiet
   with the outlet submerged. Quiet = sealed. Continuous trickle = not sealed — reseat
   the gasket/bottle.

Level checks are by eye from outside — the bottle is the gauge. Swap when empty; bees
empty the tray remainder themselves.

## Removal / season end

Unscrew bottle → screw in plug (hive stays bee-tight) → or remove entirely: spin nut off,
drop body out, push a wine cork / Ø45 disc into the hole. Wash all parts in warm water
(≤50 °C — no dishwasher); everything is open-bore and brushable. Store out of the sun.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Tray floods to the rim | leak: gasket not seated / bottle not tight / cracked print | remove bottle, reseat gasket, retighten; bench 24 h leak test the body |
| Tray bone-dry, bottle full | outlet airlocked after fill | press the bottle flank once (forces a glug) |
| Pool creeps up hot afternoons then recedes | normal thermal breathing | none needed; shade/foil the bottle if it approaches the boss tops |
| Bees not using it | access | verify under-tray gap 6–9 mm (measure headspace!), dribble syrup on the rim once |
| Feeder rotates when swapping bottles | nut not snug | re-tighten nut |
